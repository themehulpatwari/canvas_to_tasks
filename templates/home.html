What it does

Paste your Canvas calendar link once and your assignments show up in Google Tasks with their due dates. It checks Canvas once a day and adds new assignments or updates changed due dates.

Syncs daily

After you save your link, it checks Canvas once a day on its own.

Keeps up with changes

If a due date moves, the task moves with it. No duplicates.

Shows up where you already look

Your assignments appear in Google Tasks and Google Calendar, on your phone and on the web.

Your data

You can disconnect and delete everything at any time.

Setup

  1. Sign in with Google.
  2. In Canvas, go to Calendar → Calendar Feed and copy the link.
  3. Paste it here and click Sync.
  4. Your assignments appear in a new list called "dot_tasklist" in Google Tasks. Tasks show the due date but not the time, since Google Tasks doesn't let apps set one.
